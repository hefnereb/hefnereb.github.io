I thought our discussion about personal ethics was interesting because it showed how different people approach decisions that don’t have a clear right or wrong answer. It made me realize that most situations involve trade-offs, where some level of harm is often unavoidable, and people have to decide what matters more. I also enjoyed hearing everyone else’s perspectives and how different factors in their lives influenced the way they think about ethics.

One thing I noticed is that I tend to think about ethics in terms of outcomes. I usually weigh the benefits of an action against the harm it might cause and consider how it affects others. At the same time, intention still matters to me. I think why someone acts is important, but I don’t believe good intentions excuse harm if the consequences were predictable.
